Before Your Visit

Admission & Discharge

Hospital staff can guide patients and families through admission and discharge procedures. Confirm required documents, deposits, billing steps and discharge instructions with the relevant hospital desk.

Insurance & Cashless Services

Patients should be treated with dignity, receive understandable information about their care and have their privacy protected. Patients and attendants should follow hospital safety instructions and provide accurate information to the care team.

HAVE QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find basic information about appointments, documents, insurance and contacting the hospital.

Ask a Question
How do I book an appointment?

Call the official reception number or submit the website's appointment request form demonstration. Contact the hospital directly to confirm availability and booking.

How can I check a doctor's availability?

Contact reception to confirm the doctor's current schedule, speciality, consultation timings and appointment requirements.

What should I bring for my visit?

Carry relevant previous reports, prescriptions and any documents requested by the hospital. Confirm special preparation instructions before travelling.

Does the hospital accept insurance or cashless treatment?

Contact the hospital insurance desk to verify current insurer and TPA partners, eligibility, documentation and authorisation requirements.

How do I apply for a job?

Contact the hospital to ask about current vacancies and follow the official application instructions for the relevant advertised role.

How can I share feedback or make a complaint?

Contact the hospital and request the approved patient-relations or grievance contact and complaint-handling process.

Can I use this website for a medical emergency?

No. Website forms and email are not emergency channels. Contact emergency services or seek immediate medical care.
